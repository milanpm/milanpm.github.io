# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Alex Tech Blog respects the privacy of its visitors.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website and how that information may be used.

## Information Collection

This website does not directly require visitors to provide personal information in order to read its content.

However, third-party services used by this website may automatically collect certain technical information, such as:

- Browser type
- Device information
- IP address
- Referring pages
- Pages visited
- Date and time of access

This information may be used for website operation, security, analytics, and service improvement.

## Cookies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies provided by third-party services.

Cookies are small files stored on a visitor's device and may be used to remember preferences, analyze website traffic, or provide relevant advertising.

Visitors can control or disable cookies through their browser settings.

This website may use Google AdSense in the future to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's previous visits to this website or other websites.

If advertising services are enabled, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect the services being used.

## Third-Party Services

This website may use or link to third-party services, including GitHub and other technology platforms.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies and data handling practices.

Alex Tech Blog is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

## External Links

Articles on this website may contain links to external websites.

Visitors should review the privacy policies of those websites when leaving Alex Tech Blog.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, analytics tools, advertising services, or applicable requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page.
